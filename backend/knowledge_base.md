# Penguin Store Official Knowledge Base & Policies

## Return & Refund Policy
- Customers can request a full refund within 14 days of delivery for all IN_HOUSE products.
- Items must be unused, in their original packaging, and accompanied by the receipt.
- DROPSHIP items are subject to our international partner inspection and carry a 30-day return window with a small restocking fee.

## Warranty & Repairs
- All electronic items (such as the Quantum Pro Wireless Headphones and laptops) include a 1-year official Penguin Store warranty covering manufacturing defects.
- Accessories and kitchen items carry a 6-month limited warranty.

## Payment & Checkout Security
- Penguin Store accepts major credit/debit cards via Stripe, Apple Pay, and secure cash-on-delivery options for local warehouse shipments.
- All transactions are encrypted with 256-bit SSL security.

## Store Fulfillment Locations
- IN_HOUSE products are dispatched directly from our automated fulfillment center in Sargodha, Pakistan.
- DROPSHIP products are sourced from certified global partners across international hubs.
